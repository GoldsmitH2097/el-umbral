Privacy Policy

Data controller

Soulware Editorial. Contact: editorial@soulware.live

What we collect

We only collect data you explicitly provide through the contact form: name, email address, and message. We do not collect data automatically and we do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Purpose of processing

Data provided through the contact form is used exclusively to respond to your enquiry. We do not share it with third parties or use it for commercial mailings without your prior consent.

Legal basis

Processing is based on your express consent when submitting the form (Art. 6.1.a GDPR).

Retention

Data is kept only for the time strictly necessary to handle your enquiry and, in any case, no longer than 12 months from the last communication.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, delete, object to, and restrict the processing of your data. To exercise these rights, write to editorial@soulware.live. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Contact form

The contact form uses Formspree to process submissions. You can read their privacy policy at formspree.io.
