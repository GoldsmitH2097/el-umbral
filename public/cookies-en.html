Cookie Policy

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device when you visit them. They allow the site to remember information about your visit.

Cookies we use

Soulware.live does not use tracking, advertising, or third-party analytics cookies. The site does not install cookies on your device to track your activity or serve personalised advertising.

Only technical local storage strictly necessary for the basic functioning of the interactive experience may be used (for example, to save session state). This storage contains no personal information and is not shared with third parties.

Third-party cookies

The contact form uses Formspree, which may set its own technical cookies to process the form. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other third-party tracking tool.

Controlling cookies

You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies. See your browser's help for more information. Note that blocking all cookies may affect the functioning of some websites.

Changes to this policy

If in the future we introduce features that involve the use of cookies, we will update this policy and implement a consent system in line with applicable regulations.
