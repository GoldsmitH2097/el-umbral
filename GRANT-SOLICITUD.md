## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION *(el campo importante — pegar tal cual)*

Soulware is an independent Spanish publishing house specialising in
adult-oriented dark fiction. Our storefront, soulware.live, is not a
catalogue — it is a cinematic, immersive experience where four author
"archetypes" and their universes live.

Our flagship ElevenLabs use case is Tizno, the Custodian of the Threshold: a
tiny, skittish goblin-like creature who lives in the fog of our website and
speaks with visitors through ElevenLabs Agents. Tizno is fully bilingual
(Spanish/English, enforced per session), runs on an instantly-cloned voice,
and blends three ElevenLabs capabilities into one character: (1) live
conversation via Agents with RAG over our lore knowledge base, (2) a bank of
34+ pre-generated emotional voice takes created with Eleven v3 audio tags
(gasps, growls, pleas — they play as he reacts physically to the visitor's
cursor and touch), and (3) planned long-form narration: our next release,
"Anatomía del Vacío", is an interactive line-by-line reading experience that
will be fully voiced with ElevenLabs TTS.

Tizno is live in production today (soulware.live/tizno) with real usage:
125+ conversations, 99%+ session success rate, ~2.2s median latency. He is
our reader-acquisition engine: he guards the mystery of our books, remembers
returning visitors (locally, privacy-first — no cookies, no tracking), and
captures release-notification sign-ups. Revenue comes from book sales
(available now via Amazon, Fnac, Casa del Libro and El Corte Inglés) with
premium interactive fiction planned. The grant would let us scale Tizno from
a strictly rate-limited demo economy to the always-available character our
readers are already falling in love with, and to voice our entire
interactive-fiction roadmap with ElevenLabs.
